About Kamel Money

Founded in 2024

Our Mission

Kamel Money was created with one simple mission: to help immigrants and expats from the MENA region, South Asia, and West Africa send money home without losing it to hidden fees and poor exchange rates.

Every year, millions of people work hard abroad to support their families. Traditional banks and some transfer services charge up to 10% in total costs when you combine fees and FX margins. We believe that money should reach your family, not get lost in the system.

What We Do

Kamel Money is a free, independent comparison tool. We instantly compare live fees, exchange rates, and transfer speeds from 7 major remittance providers including Wise, Remitly, Revolut, WorldRemit, Ria, MoneyGram, and Western Union.

Our algorithm calculates the exact amount your recipient will receive after all fees and FX markups. We sort results by "Cheapest" by default, but you can also sort by "Fastest" or "Best rate" depending on your needs.

How We Make Money

Kamel Money is free for you to use. We may receive a small affiliate commission if you choose to complete a transfer through one of our partner links. This commission comes from the provider, not from you.

Important: This does not affect our rankings. Our results are sorted purely by math: the provider that gives your recipient the most money is always ranked #1. We do not accept payment for placement.

Our Data

Exchange rates are indicative and updated hourly from reliable financial data sources. Fees are based on the public pricing models of each provider for online transfers. We work hard to keep our data accurate, but we always recommend confirming the final rate and fees on the provider's official website before you send.

Kamel Money is not a bank, money transfer service, or financial advisor. We are an information service only.

Who We Are

Kamel Money is an independent project built by a small team with roots in the diaspora. We understand the problem because we've lived it. Our goal is to build the most transparent and useful tool for our community.
