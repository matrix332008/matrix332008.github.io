About Kamel Money

Kamel Money was created to solve one simple problem: sending money abroad is too expensive and confusing.

Our Mission

Millions of people send money to support family in countries like Tunisia, Morocco, Egypt, Algeria, and across Africa and Asia. Traditional banks and some transfer services charge high hidden fees and give poor exchange rates. We believe you deserve transparency.

How It Works

Kamel Money compares 7 trusted remittance operators in real-time: Wise, Remitly, Revolut, WorldRemit, Ria, MoneyGram, and Western Union. Our calculator shows you the exact fees, the real exchange rate, and the final amount your recipient will get. We update rates automatically using live mid-market data.

100% Free & Independent

This tool is completely free to use. We do not charge you anything. We may receive a small commission if you choose to send money via one of our partner links, but this never affects the price you pay or our rankings. We show you the cheapest option first, always.

Not a Financial Institution

Important: Kamel Money is an information and comparison service only. We are not a bank, money transmitter, or financial advisor. We do not hold your money or process transfers. Always confirm the final fees and rates on the provider's official website before sending.
